A training runs while nobody uses the card: any request pauses it, and it goes on from its last checkpoint once the router has been idle a minute. It runs with no network, read-only, as you, over its own folder.

Train a voice mnm studio train voice <name> --from <folder> --source …

A folder of the voice's recordings on this machine: 10 to 60 minutes of the voice alone and dry, WAV or FLAC, is what RVC voices are trained on (less trains, and sounds less like them). The recordings are copied into the training's folder and kept on the voice by their hashes; a consent document is kept only as its hash.

Style trainer mnm studio trainer install --for styles · status · rollback · uninstall

Optional: ACE-Step 1.5's own trainer, built on this machine, teaches ACE-Step a style from your songs: a LoRA that an ACE-Step instance plays (mnm set <instance> style=<name>). On a card under 16 GB it trains with the tight settings, and its first epoch measures what the training itself holds of the card: too near the top on its own, it stops with what to change; taken there by other programs' share, it pauses until that room is free.

Train a style mnm studio train style <name> --from <folder> --caption … --source …

A folder of songs on this machine: five or more, 30 seconds to 4 minutes each, WAV or FLAC keep the sound; a <song>.txt beside a song is its lyrics. The caption says what the songs sound like; the trigger word (the name unless given) goes in front of it, and a song's style line carries it afterwards. ACE-Step's checkpoint for the build is downloaded the first time (about 6 GB).
