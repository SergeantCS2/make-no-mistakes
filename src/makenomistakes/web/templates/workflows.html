Sets

A set is a group of instances with a role each (a chat model, a coder, an image model, a voice …) that starts and stops together and is planned to fit together on this card. With take turns, members that don't fit together share the card: a request to a parked member loads it and parks the others. The set's page gathers every member's URLs for one app.
